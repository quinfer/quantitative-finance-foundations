# Exercises

The curriculum contains nine tasks and three projects. Choose a task from the phase you are working on, then use the relevant reading guide for additional exercises and checks. The task descriptions in the curriculum are the starting point for independent work. [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Reading guides](../resources/)

For each task, keep a short record of the question, data and method alongside your code. Show one calculation you checked and state one assumption that affects the result. Give the commands needed to reproduce your tables or figures. [How to assess your work](../CURRICULUM.md#assess-your-work)
